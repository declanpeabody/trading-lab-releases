Positions now includes closed-position history: entry and sale dates, shares sold, entry/sale prices, cost basis, net proceeds, realised GBP and percentage return, and exit reason. Repeated holdings are separate records, with 20 rows per page and aggregate recorded realised performance. History includes all stored sale executions, independently of the recent-execution limit. Older unrecorded sales are not reconstructed. Trading rules and data budgets are unchanged.
