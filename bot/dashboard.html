Scanner

Scanner coverage

The scanner targets 300 supported NASDAQ/NYSE common stocks. It retains the original 30 where listed and fills the rest using a stable catalogue sample; this is not a top-300 index. Score = 20-session return divided by daily volatility, with a 1% volatility floor. Trend and volume filters must pass. Holdings and up to six ready candidates get fresh quotes first. Afterwards, up to 20 additional histories are downloaded for the next check. Histories must share a completed-session grid verified today. When no new session has completed, matching cached histories can be reused. A new session still needs fresh histories. Unavailable stocks are skipped individually. A full 300-stock download needs at least 15 batches and may span multiple sessions if quota or uptime is limited.

Waiting for the first regular-hours scan.

Local API attempts are capped at 500/day for scanning and historical research, with 200 more reserved for held-stock checks. Other apps using your key are not counted. Exhausting the provider quota can still stop all monitoring. Historical research remains a fixed 30-stock baseline. It does not replay the expanded universe, batch order or partial coverage.
