Scanner

Scanner coverage

The scanner targets 300 supported NASDAQ/NYSE common stocks. It retains the original 30 where listed and fills the rest using a stable catalogue sample; this is not a top-300 index. Score = 20-session return divided by daily volatility, with a 1% volatility floor. Trend and volume filters must pass. Holdings and the exchange rate are checked first. Optional candidate quotes follow only when the active account or enabled experiments need them. New entries require all valuation quotes to remain fresh; a candidate failure cannot undo a completed holdings check. Afterwards, up to 5 histories are prepared: up to four from a 50-name daily priority list and one from the wider universe. Once priority names are processed, spare slots rotate more widely. The daily list retains the original 30 and favours the highest available historical dollar volume for the other 20; unknown histories use alphabetical order. This is a download priority, not a guarantee of liquidity or a top-50 index. All ready eligible stocks can still enter the ranking. A sale triggers a candidate check without the usual 30-minute wait, subject to normal credits and fresh quotes. Histories must share a completed-session grid verified today. When no new session has completed, matching cached histories can be reused. A new session still needs fresh histories. Unavailable stocks are skipped individually. A full 300-stock download needs at least 60 batches and may span multiple sessions if quota or uptime is limited.
